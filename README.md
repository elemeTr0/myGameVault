# GameCollector 🎮

**Your gaming collection, organized in one place.**

GameCollector is a video game collection and backlog tracker inspired by Goodreads. The goal is to provide a place where gamers can discover games, organize their collections, and keep track of their gaming journey.

## Planned Features

- **Game Discovery** – Browse a large catalog of video games powered by IGDB.
- **Game Details** – Explore game information, cover artwork, ratings, and release dates.
- **Personal Collection** – Build and manage your own video game library.
- **Backlog Tracking** – Keep track of games you want to play.
- **Progress Tracking** – Organize games by their playing status and completion progress.
